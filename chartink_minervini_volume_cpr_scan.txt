Mark Minervini Volume + Virgin/Narrow CPR — Chartink custom scan
================================================================

Use this as a Chartink custom scan (Stock scanner → Create scan → Custom scan).
Adjust the watchlist to Nifty 500 / your universe. Daily timeframe.

Section 1 — Minervini Trend Template with Volume
------------------------------------------------
(close >= ema(close, 150))
and (close >= ema(close, 200))
and (ema(close, 150) >= ema(close, 200))
and (ema(close, 200) > ema(close, 200, 21 days ago))
and (ema(close, 50) > ema(close, 150))
and (ema(close, 50) > ema(close, 200))
and (close > ema(close, 50))
and (close >= 1.3 * lowest(low, 252))
and (close >= 0.75 * highest(high, 252))
and (volume >= ema(volume, 20))

Section 2 — Virgin CPR Daily Buy
--------------------------------
Virgin above prior pivot (Chartink "Virgin CPR" bullish variant):
  (low > (1 day ago high + 1 day ago low + 1 day ago close) / 3)

Daily CPR buy breakout:
and (1 day ago close <= 1 day ago cpr top)
and (close > cpr top)
and (close > open)
and (volume >= ema(volume, 20))

Optional stricter virgin (use --virgin-use-full-cpr in Python):
  (low > 1 day ago cpr top)

Section 3 — Narrow CPR High-Probability Breakout
-----------------------------------------------
Narrow CPR (width as % of close; default 0.5% in Python, Chartink EOD uses 0.1%):
  ((cpr top - cpr bottom) / close <= 0.005)

Breakout confirmation:
and (1 day ago close <= 1 day ago cpr top)
and (close > cpr top)
and (close > open)
and (volume >= ema(volume, 20))

CPR math (daily, from prior session OHLC)
-----------------------------------------
pivot  = (prev high + prev low + prev close) / 3
bc     = (prev high + prev low) / 2
tc     = 2 * pivot - bc
cpr top    = max(tc, bc)
cpr bottom = min(tc, bc)

Notes
-----
- Chartink cannot combine all three sections in one saved scan as flexibly as the
  Python scanner; run sections separately or use score mode in Python.
- Tighten narrow CPR by lowering --narrow-cpr-max-width-pct (e.g. 0.001 = 0.1%).
- For a broader list, run Section 1 only in Chartink and export hits to the repo
  scanner for virgin/narrow CPR validation.
